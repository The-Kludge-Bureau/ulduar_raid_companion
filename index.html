How to read this

  • Mechanics described are patch 3.3.5a as scripted on AzerothCore, which is the core ChromieCraft runs. Private server scripting can differ from retail in small ways, so treat unusual timings on your first pull as a scripting difference rather than a mistake.
  • Class advice assumes standard Wrath talent trees and the abilities available at level 80 in 3.3.5a. Anything that arrived in Cataclysm or later is deliberately absent.
  • Where sources disagreed on an exact number, the ability is described by what it does rather than by a figure that might be wrong on your server.
  • Ability damage values are omitted almost everywhere on purpose. They vary by raid size, gear and server tuning, and knowing what to do about a mechanic matters more than knowing its tooltip.
  • Enrage timers and the attempt-ending mechanics are taken from the AzerothCore encounter scripts, because that is the core ChromieCraft runs, and cross-checked against Warcraft Wiki and Icy Veins. Where the core and the wikis disagree the entry says so. Several of these differ from retail, so a retail guide will mislead you on Ignis, Razorscale, Kologarn and 10 player Firefighter in particular.
